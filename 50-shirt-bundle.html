Before you order

Quick answers.

What is included in the $250 package?

50 Gildan Softstyle 64000 short-sleeve shirts in sizes S–XL with a 1-color front print, a free digital mockup, and artwork review. 2XL and 3XL are +$3 per shirt. Printing is front only, with an optional 1-color back print for +$1 per shirt.

Can I mix shirt sizes?

Yes. Split the 50 shirts across S, M, L and XL at the package price. 2XL and 3XL are available for +$3 per shirt. Our size guide can help you plan the split.

Can I split the order between multiple shirt colors?

Yes. Two shirt colors are included. Orders using three or more colors add $0.75 per shirt.

What if my logo is not print-ready?

Upload the best file you have. A real person reviews the artwork and helps prepare it for production. See our artwork guide for preferred file types.

Can I print a multi-color or full-color design?

No. This pack is for one-color designs only. Your artwork is printed in a single ink color, so if your logo has several colors, tell us which ink color you want and we prep it that way.

How long does it take?

Standard turnaround is 5–7 business days after you approve the mockup. Rush orders are not available on the 50-Shirt Pack. The clock starts once details, artwork and any price adjustments are confirmed.

Do I pay anything to get a quote?

No. You'll receive a mockup to approve first, and payment is only collected after you approve.
